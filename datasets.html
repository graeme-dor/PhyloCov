Datasets

Covariate families relevant to transmission ecology

PhyloCov is built for variable discovery across environmental drivers that may shape persistence, dispersal, suitability, seasonal forcing, and contact structure in host-pathogen systems.

Climate

Temperature, precipitation, climate surfaces

Core climate drivers for thermal suitability, rainfall-linked dynamics, and seasonal forcing in host, vector, and pathogen systems.

Landscape

NDVI, land cover, vegetation context

Ecological context layers for greenness, habitat structure, and land-use heterogeneity that can shape persistence, suitability, and movement.

Population

Population density and human context

Human population surfaces that can support contact structure hypotheses, exposure context, and transmission modelling in anthropogenic settings.

Topography

Elevation, slope, and suitability layers

Static terrain constraints plus custom assets such as vector suitability layers for ecologically informed covariate construction.
