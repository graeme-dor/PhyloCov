About

Designed for ecological covariate discovery in phylodynamics

Research context

PhyloCov is positioned as a discovery layer for climate and ecological variables that can support pathogen spread analysis, risk mapping, and transmission modelling.

Why this structure works

Separating the public website from the Earth Engine app reduces deployment friction, keeps the interface maintainable, and lets you present the science and the tool together.

What you can add next

Dataset notes, citations, changelogs, project team pages, user guides, and a catalog of multiple Earth Engine tools can all be layered onto this shell.
