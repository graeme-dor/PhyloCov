Ecological covariates for phylodynamics

Explore transmission-relevant environmental context through a live Earth Engine interface.

PhyloCov is a research-facing web layer around a live Google Earth Engine application for exploring ecological covariates relevant to pathogen spread, transmission modelling, and integrated phylodynamic analysis.

Research framing

Why this platform exists.

Ecological and climatic variables are often central to phylodynamic inference, but they are frequently difficult to discover, compare, and prepare consistently for model-ready use.

Purpose
Covariate discovery and extraction
Application
Live embedded Earth Engine app
Use case
Phylodynamics and transmission modelling

Story

From environmental surfaces to phylodynamic inputs

PhyloCov is intended as an interpretive layer between large environmental data catalogues and downstream inference. The goal is not just to display maps, but to help researchers move from ecological intuition to transparent covariate selection.

01

Identify ecologically plausible drivers

Screen climate, vegetation, land-use, population, topography, and imported suitability layers against a focal transmission question before building formal hypotheses.

02

Configure extraction deliberately

Define region, country, time window, spatial resolution, and summary settings so the resulting covariates match the scale and assumptions of the downstream model.

03

Carry context into analysis

Use the interactive workspace to inspect outputs, compare surfaces, and preserve dataset provenance before exporting inputs into transmission or phylodynamic workflows.

“
