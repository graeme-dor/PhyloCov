About

Designed for ecological covariate discovery in phylodynamics

PhyloCov is positioned as a discovery layer for climate and ecological variables that can support pathogen spread analysis, risk mapping, and integrated phylodynamic modelling.

Research context

PhyloCov brings climate and ecological covariate exploration into a more accessible research-facing interface around a live Earth Engine application.

Why the app stays separate

Separating the public website from the Earth Engine app reduces deployment friction, keeps the interface maintainable, and lets the science and the tool evolve together without being tightly coupled.

Model-facing design

The site can carry dataset notes, methods, citations, changelogs, and app guidance without overloading the Earth Engine interface itself.

Roadmap

Near-term platform direction

Expanded covariate library

Add broader collections of environmental surfaces, imported assets, and grouped covariate presets that align with common modelling use cases.

API and programmatic access

Expose more structured outputs and documented access patterns so PhyloCov can sit more cleanly inside reproducible analysis pipelines.

Related tools

Leave room for companion applications such as pathogen- or workflow-specific modules that can be surfaced from the same hosting shell.
