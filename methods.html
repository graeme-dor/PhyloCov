Methods

How PhyloCov fits into phylodynamic and transmission pipelines

The platform is designed as a front door for covariate discovery, variable justification, and reproducible handoff into downstream statistical or mechanistic models.

Phase A

Choose datasets and bands

Select from climate, topography, land-use, vegetation, population, and imported asset layers, then choose the relevant band or derived variable for the system of interest.

Phase B

Configure processing choices

Define region, country, temporal window, spatial resolution, and zonal summary settings so the extracted covariates match the downstream analytical design.

Phase C

Visualise and export outputs

Inspect interactive maps and summary outputs, then carry selected surfaces into environmental modelling or phylodynamic workflows with transparent provenance.

Interpretation

How the outputs are intended to be used

1

Screen biologically plausible variables

Build a shortlist of candidate covariates that align with plausible host, vector, or pathogen ecology.

2

Link variables to hypotheses

Align climate and ecological signals with mechanistic expectations such as persistence, movement, or seasonal forcing.

3

Carry provenance into reporting

Keep track of datasets, settings, and transformations so downstream modelling decisions remain reproducible.
